# Product Acceptance Matrix — Whitelist V1 Remediation

A technically green artifact is not sufficient. This matrix is required before production authorization.

## Viewports
- 360px
- 390px
- 768px
- 1024px
- 1440px
- 200% text zoom

## Global shell
- Exactly one intended H1 per canonical page.
- No clipped text, overlap, hidden actionable content, or page-level horizontal overflow.
- Header geometry remains stable and does not obscure anchors/content.
- Keyboard focus order is logical; Escape/focus-return behavior works where applicable.
- Mobile interactive targets are at least 44px effective size.
- Console errors: 0.
- Axe serious/critical: 0.

## Homepage
- First screen identifies Bitmomo as a BTC market-intelligence / research product.
- Current BTC intelligence or its fail-closed state is understandable without reading methodology.
- Product proof precedes secondary content/roadmap.
- Founding whitelist is clear, honest, and not visually confused with newsletter/referral actions.
- Research shown on homepage is institutionally qualified.

## BTC Intelligence
- First substantive read follows State -> Change -> Meaning -> Watch.
- Market Pulse and Major Assessment clocks cannot be confused.
- Unavailable/degraded assessment explains the quality/freshness boundary rather than looking broken.
- Market Context chart is readable, responsive, keyboard/touch inspectable, and fail-closed.
- History + Decision Ledger + Track Record read as one coherent accountability surface.
- All matured evidence remains inspectable; presentation must not cherry-pick outcomes.
- Methodology remains available but is secondary to current intelligence.

## Pro
- Real delayed/frozen product proof appears before roadmap-heavy explanation when eligible evidence exists.
- Public surfaces never query or leak current protected Pro values.
- Free vs Pro distinction is understandable in one scan.
- Founding pricing/cap/whitelist semantics remain factual.
- Future capabilities are clearly marked as not-live.

## Footer / navigation
- Footer communicates Product / Research / Bitmomo information architecture.
- Track Record is visitor-facing terminology; internal jargon is not primary navigation.
- Research destinations distinguish Market Research, AI Research, and Research Standard.
- Newsletter is visually secondary to the commercial Founding action.
- Social and Terms links remain fail-closed when not valid/published.

## Research / articles
- Generic legacy Riset is not the primary institutional research destination.
- Qualified research classification is consistent across hub, article and navigation.
- Article body remains comfortably readable on mobile and desktop.
- No legacy newsletter/template/disclaimer typography pollution appears inside authored research body.

## Whitelist
- Existing consent, validation, normalized dedupe, persistence and confirmation behavior remains unchanged.
- Checkout stays OFF for this release.
- WhatsApp stays OFF for this release.
- Success state provides a useful continuation path and does not create a dead end.

## Human acceptance
Final staging screenshots and interactive review must be explicitly accepted by product/owner. If product acceptance fails, do not promote even if automated gates are green.
