# Bitmomo Institutional Copy System V1

## Purpose

This document is the canonical editorial contract for visitor-facing Bitmomo copy. It exists to keep product language finance-grade, natural in Bahasa Indonesia, internally consistent, and free from engineering or marketing jargon that weakens trust.

## Core language policy

Bitmomo uses Bahasa Indonesia for explanatory sentences and reserves English for product names and market terms that are standard in professional market practice.

### Product names that remain English

- BTC Intelligence
- Bitmomo Pro
- Decision Ledger
- Expected Range
- Scenario Map
- What Changed

### Market terms that may remain English

- bullish / bearish / neutral
- funding rate
- basis
- open interest
- breakout / breakdown
- spot / futures
- ETF
- market structure

### Indonesian equivalents required in explanatory prose

- evidence -> bukti
- thesis -> tesis
- outcome -> hasil
- history -> riwayat
- feedback -> masukan
- support -> bantuan
- uncertainty -> ketidakpastian
- evaluation -> evaluasi
- reliability -> keandalan
- provenance -> asal-usul data / sumber data, depending on context

## Information hierarchy

1. DATA — observable market facts.
2. ASSESSMENT — bias, confidence, and market factors derived from current evidence.
3. SCENARIO — expected range, base/bull/bear conditions, and thesis invalidation.
4. ACCOUNTABILITY — actual results, evaluation, and public ledger evidence.

Copy must not blur these levels. Descriptive free intelligence must not imply scenario guidance that belongs to Pro.

## Tone

Use precise, restrained, declarative language. Prefer terms used by professional research desks and market-data products. Avoid conversational filler, hype, self-congratulation, or defensive language.

### Avoid in public copy

- alasan utama
- history palsu
- dibocorkan
- cukup kuat ke arah...
- apa berikutnya?
- institutional research as a self-congratulatory claim
- fail-closed, quality gate, capability, layer, black box, or other engineering jargon unless the page is explicitly technical research
- mixed Indonesian-English prose when a natural Indonesian equivalent exists

## Canonical interface terminology

- CURRENT BTC READING -> BTC MARKET VIEW
- ARAH -> BIAS
- KEYAKINAN -> CONFIDENCE
- ALASAN UTAMA -> FAKTOR UTAMA
- Buka pembacaan lengkap -> Buka analisis lengkap
- Arah evidence pasar saat ini. -> Arah dominan berdasarkan data pasar saat ini.
- Konsistensi evidence, bukan peluang harga. -> Konsistensi bukti pendukung; bukan probabilitas pergerakan harga.

## Dynamic market-driver style

Prefer concise market language:

- Momentum harga BTC saat ini cukup kuat ke arah turun. -> Momentum harga menunjukkan tekanan bearish yang kuat.
- Momentum harga BTC saat ini cukup kuat ke arah naik. -> Momentum harga menunjukkan dorongan bullish yang kuat.
- Momentum dan struktur harga BTC saat ini sama-sama menunjukkan tekanan ke arah turun. -> Momentum dan struktur harga sama-sama mengonfirmasi bias bearish.
- Momentum dan struktur harga BTC saat ini sama-sama mendukung arah naik. -> Momentum dan struktur harga sama-sama mengonfirmasi bias bullish.
- Struktur harga BTC baru saja menembus level penting ke bawah. -> Struktur harga mencatat breakdown di bawah level teknikal utama.
- Struktur harga BTC baru saja menembus level penting ke atas. -> Struktur harga mencatat breakout di atas level teknikal utama.

## Commercial language

Commercial CTAs should be clear without urgency theater.

- KUNCI HARGA FOUNDING -> AKTIFKAN FOUNDING MEMBERSHIP
- Founding price terkunci selama membership tetap aktif. -> Founding Price berlaku selama membership tetap aktif.

## Editorial acceptance

Visitor-facing copy must pass the following before release:

- natural Bahasa Indonesia;
- finance-grade terminology;
- no accidental internal-engineering language;
- no unnecessary English inside Indonesian sentences;
- claims remain bounded by available data;
- free/pro information boundary remains intact;
- legal meaning is unchanged;
- labels, tooltips, empty states, error states, meta descriptions, and dynamic generated copy are included in review.
